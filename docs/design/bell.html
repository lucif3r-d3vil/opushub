The bell

One panel for everything that needs you, and a record of what happened. It replaces the Activity page. Click the bell in the top bar for the real popover; below are four states, all interactive.

State 1

Needs you

  • Unacknowledged items first, each with a severity dot, one plain sentence and one to three actions.
  • The paper-plane mark says it also went to Telegram.
  • Acknowledge dims the row and moves it to "Acknowledged"; the badge count in the top bar drops.

State 2

Filtered to Auto-heal

  • Filters narrow both lists: Needs you, Updates, Auto-heal, Downtime.
  • Activity rows expand on click to show the detail behind them, including your own actions (the audit record).

State 3

All clear

  • When nothing needs you the panel says so and the badge disappears.
  • Recent activity stays, so "what just happened?" always has an answer.

State 4

On a phone (360 px)

  • Same panel, full width under the top bar. Actions wrap rather than shrink.
  • Closes with Esc, a tap outside, or the bell again; focus returns to the bell.
